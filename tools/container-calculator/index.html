How the numbers are worked out

  • Enter packed sizes — crate, skid and protection included, not the bare equipment.
  • Vehicle figures are internal usable dimensions. Door openings are smaller than the box, so check the tallest item against the door height before you book.
  • The packer places large items first and fills from the nose backwards. It is a fast heuristic, not a proof of the theoretical minimum — treat the answer as a solid working estimate to confirm with your forwarder.
  • Clearance is the gap kept between neighbouring items along the length and width. It is not kept to the walls, and not between stacked tiers. Sizes in the piece list are the sizes you entered; positions include the clearance.
  • Centre of gravity is reported as a percentage of the vehicle length from the nose. The 45–55% band is a rule of thumb for an evenly loaded vehicle, not a figure from a standard; outside it, check axle loads and lashing with your forwarder.
  • The PDF report prints Latin letters only. Other scripts in a project name or tag print as “[non-Latin text]” there; the Excel files keep them as typed.
  • A list of more than 5,000 pieces is not calculated: split it into shipments first.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Files you open are read locally and never sent anywhere.
